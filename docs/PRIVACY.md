# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 2024**

## Introduction

CoolRides ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information:
- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Profile photo
- Location data (when using the app)

### Rider Information:
- Ghana ID
- Driver's license details
- Vehicle information
- Banking/Mobile Money details

### Usage Data:
- Trip history
- Payment records
- GPS location during trips
- App usage patterns

## How We Use Your Information

- **Service Delivery**: Match riders with passengers, process payments
- **Safety**: Verify identities, track trips, handle emergencies
- **Improvement**: Analyze usage to improve service
- **Communication**: Send trip updates, promotional offers
- **Legal Compliance**: Meet regulatory requirements

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:
- With riders/passengers for trip completion
- With payment processors
- With law enforcement when legally required
- With service providers under confidentiality agreements

## Location Data

- Collected only during active trips
- Used for navigation and safety
- Can be disabled when not using the service
- Retained for trip history and support

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Encryption of sensitive data
- Secure servers
- Regular security audits
- Access controls

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request data deletion
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Download your data

## Data Retention

- Active account data: Retained while account is active
- Trip history: Retained for 7 years for legal compliance
- Deleted account data: Removed within 90 days (except legal requirements)

## Children's Privacy

CoolRides is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:
- Authentication
- Preferences
- Analytics
- Security

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy and will notify users of significant changes.
