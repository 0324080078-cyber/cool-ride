# Rider Agreement

**Last Updated: December 2024**

## Agreement to Drive with CoolRides

This agreement outlines the terms for operating as a Keke (Pragia) rider on the CoolRides platform.

## 1. Eligibility Requirements

To drive with CoolRides, you must:
- Be at least 21 years old
- Hold a valid Ghana driver's license
- Possess a valid Ghana ID
- Own or lease a registered Keke/Pragia
- Have valid vehicle insurance
- Pass background verification

## 2. Vehicle Requirements

Your Keke must:
- Be properly registered
- Have valid insurance
- Be in good working condition
- Meet safety standards
- Accommodate passengers comfortably (up to 3 passengers)

## 3. Driver Responsibilities

### Service Quality:
- Provide safe, courteous service
- Maintain clean vehicle
- Follow traffic laws
- Use designated routes when possible
- Arrive on time for pickups

### Documentation:
- Keep licenses and insurance current
- Update vehicle information promptly
- Report accidents immediately

### Conduct:
- Treat passengers with respect
- No smoking in vehicle
- No discrimination
- Professional appearance

## 4. Earnings and Payments

### Fare Structure:
- **Daytime (6 AM - 10 PM)**: Base GHS 3 + distance/time
- **Nighttime (10 PM - 3 AM)**: GHS 15-20 based on distance

### Payment:
- CoolRides retains 20% service fee
- Payments processed weekly to Mobile Money or bank account
- Trip earnings visible in real-time
- Bonuses for high ratings and completed trips

### Cancellations:
- Reasonable cancellation fee if passenger cancels late
- Penalties for rider-initiated cancellations

## 5. Rating System

- Maintain minimum 4.0 star rating
- Low ratings may result in:
  - Additional training requirements
  - Temporary suspension
  - Account termination

## 6. Insurance and Liability

- Riders are independent contractors
- Must maintain adequate insurance
- CoolRides not liable for accidents or injuries
- Report all incidents within 24 hours

## 7. Safety Requirements

### For Your Safety:
- Verify passenger identity
- Report suspicious activity
- Use in-app SOS features if needed
- Follow safety protocols

### For Passenger Safety:
- Drive safely and defensively
- Follow GPS directions
- Complete trips as booked
- Don't ask for off-app payments

## 8. Account Suspension/Termination

Grounds for suspension or termination:
- Safety violations
- Low ratings
- Fraudulent activity
- Expired documentation
- Customer complaints
- Terms violation

## 9. Working Hours

- Set your own schedule
- Toggle online/offline as needed
- No minimum hours required
- Peak hours incentives available

## 10. Support and Training

CoolRides provides:
- Initial onboarding training
- Ongoing support
- Safety resources
- Technical assistance

## 11. Independent Contractor Status

- You are an independent contractor, not an employee
- Responsible for your own taxes
- No employment benefits provided
- Freedom to accept/reject trips

## 12. App Usage

- Must use CoolRides app for all bookings
- Accept reasonable number of trip requests
- Update location regularly when online
- Maintain working smartphone

## 13. Background Checks

- Initial background check required
- Periodic re-verification
- Criminal record check
- License verification

## 14. Bonuses and Incentives

Earn extra through:
- High ratings bonuses
- Peak hours incentives
- Referral bonuses
- Completion milestones

## 15. Prohibited Activities

Never:
- Request off-app payments
- Share personal contact information
- Refuse service based on discrimination
- Use vehicle for illegal activities
- Tamper with GPS or app

## 16. Agreement Modifications

CoolRides may modify this agreement with notice. Continued driving constitutes acceptance.
